Flock Off Privacy Policy

Effective November 3, 2026 · Applies to the Flock Off app for Android and iOS and its report server

Short version: Flock Off uses your location on your phone to warn you about license plate readers, speed cameras and speed traps, including while the app is closed. Your location is not uploaded, stored on a server, sold or shared. The only things that leave your phone are the reports you choose to send, with no account or identity attached.

Who we are

Flock Off is made by Glitch'd. Contact: https://github.com/Kaschak-Innovations/flock-off. Flock Off is not affiliated with Flock Safety or any camera operator.

What the app does with your location

With your permission, the app reads your phone's location, including in the background when the app is closed or not in use, and compares it with camera locations stored on the phone. When you are near one, the phone shows a notification. This is the app's core feature and the reason it asks for background location access ("Allow all the time" on Android, "Always" on iPhone).

  • Your location is processed on your phone only. It is never sent to our server or anyone else by the app.
  • The app keeps a local history of the alerts it showed you (the camera's location, distance and time) so you can review them. That history stays on your phone and you can clear it by deleting the app.
  • If you turn location access off, the app stops warning you. Nothing else changes.

The app has no account system, no advertising, no analytics and no third-party tracking SDKs.

The report server

Reports are anonymous. The server stores the reported spot, its kind, timestamps, confirmation and "gone" votes, and the hashed install ID. It does not store your position, your IP address beyond the standard short-lived logs of the hosting provider (Cloudflare), or anything that identifies you. Speed trap reports expire within hours and camera reports within weeks; expired reports are deleted automatically within a day. Rate limiting uses your IP address for the duration of the request only.

Data retention and deletion

  • Everything on your phone (camera cache, settings, alert history, your own reports) is deleted when you delete the app.
  • Reports on the server expire and are deleted as described above. Because reports carry no identity, we cannot look up which reports came from you. If you want a specific report removed sooner, contact us with its location and time.
  • OpenStreetMap notes are public contributions to OpenStreetMap and are governed by its policies; we cannot delete them for you.

Permissions the app asks for

  • Location (while using and always): to warn you about cameras, including with the app closed.
  • Notifications: to show the warnings.
  • Foreground service (Android) / background location mode (iOS): drive mode reads GPS continuously while you drive so warnings arrive ahead of the camera. Android shows a persistent "Drive mode on" notification while this runs.

Children

Flock Off is intended for licensed drivers and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect information from children.
